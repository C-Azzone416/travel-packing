# Parity with the approved prototype (#23)

`reference/prototype/tests/browser.test.mjs` is one long scripted walk through the prototype. This table maps **every
scenario in it** (by its line range) to the Playwright test that covers it on the production app, against the real
database, at phone (390×844) and desktop (1440×1000). "Replaced" means the prototype behaviour was intentionally changed
and the new behaviour is what is tested.

Specs: `dash` dashboard.spec · `create` create-flow.spec · `trip` trip-screen.spec · `review` review.spec ·
`staples` staples.spec · `settings` settings.spec · `persist` persistence.spec · `tripp` trip-persistence.spec ·
`auth` auth.spec · `shell` shell.spec · `parity` parity.spec (new, below).
